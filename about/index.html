About Poured

What Poured is and how to use it.

Sep 14, 2026

Poured is a practical journal for resin art beginners. Every guide answers one question completely: what to buy, how to pour it, or how to fix it when it goes wrong.

What you'll find here

  • Getting started — kits, supplies, and your first pours.
  • Techniques — ocean waves, jewelry, molds, colorants.
  • Troubleshooting — bubbles, sticky cures, and every other failure mode, diagnosed.

What you won't find

Sponsored product picks, 3,000-word intros before the answer, or gallery pieces with no instructions. If a guide doesn't save you a ruined pour, it doesn't belong here.

How to use the site

New to resin? Start with the three guides in Start in this order on the homepage. Stuck on something specific? Head to the guides archive and search for the symptom. New guides land weekly.
